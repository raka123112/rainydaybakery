# Art and audio provenance

- `Assets/Resources/Art/village.png`: original AI-generated hand-drawn-style dimetric coastal environment, OpenAI image model, 20 October 2026.
- `interior.png`: original AI-generated rustic bakery illustration, same session and direction.
- `characters.png`: original AI-generated transparent 3×3 sheet, rows Mira / Nami / Pak Aruna; columns front / side / back. Runtime alpha bounds crop the individual cells for consistent placement.
- `items.png`: original AI-generated transparent 4×4 atlas. Rows: baguette/croissant/cinnamon roll/sourdough; pretzel/melonpan/cream puff/coastal loaf; flour/eggs/butter/milk; honey/cinnamon/blueberries/mushrooms.
- Wood and paper UI textures, fog, rain ripples, light shaft, ambience noise and notification tone are generated procedurally by the game.
- `Fonts/Body.ttf` and `Fonts/Heading.ttf` are DejaVu Sans and DejaVu Serif, distributed with their license in `FONT-LICENSE.txt`.
- Original and revised design PDFs were supplied by the project owner.

No Lemon Cake artwork, audio, or proprietary assets were copied. Lemon Cake was a gameplay reference supplied by the user. Generated visuals are interpretations of the revised PDF's descriptions; that PDF does not include the referenced turnaround sheets or full item concept sheets.
